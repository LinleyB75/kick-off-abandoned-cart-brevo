## The checks, the same in every client

1. **Not clipped.** In Gmail, no "Message clipped, View entire message" at the bottom.
2. **Every picture shows:** the logo, the banner, the cart icon, the product picture, the four social icons, the footer logo and the badge.
3. **The words are right:** the heading `Your Cart Alex... below`, the product `Kick Off Your English Ebook CAT01`, the price `$19.99 AUD`, the quantity `1`.
4. **The button:** blue, rounded corners, `Return to Your Cart`. Hover or press and hold to see where it goes. Brevo may rewrite the address for click tracking.
5. **The footer:** the address and phone number look like the text around them, not blue underlined links, and the gold text is readable on the blue.
6. **On a phone:** no sideways scrolling.
7. **Dark mode:** the page is dark, the panels are dark and the text is light. The orange frame, the blue button and the blue footers stay as they are. Look for white blocks around pictures, and for any text that is hard to read.
8. **Anything else that looks wrong.**

Result: write `Pass`, or `Problem` with what you saw.
